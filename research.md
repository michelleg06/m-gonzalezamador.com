## Research

I am a development economist combining field experiments, network analysis, economic theory, and computational methods, including machine learning, to study human capital investment in low- and middle-income countries. My work examines how parents divide resources among their children, how social networks shape educational aspirations and responses to interventions, and how algorithms can distribute scarce healthcare resources to people who differ in risk and needs.

**Research fields:** computational economics, development economics, economics of education, social and economic networks, human capital, field and lab-in-the-field experiments, machine learning for public policy.
